Globally Trusted Manufacturer of High-Performance Industrial Packaging

AS Exports is a leading manufacturer delivering precision-engineered packaging systems designed for durability, safety, and long-term performance. Our production is driven by advanced technology, precision processes, and strict quality control to meet modern industry standards.

Our product range includes PP/HDPE woven sack bags, BOPP laminated bags, FIBC (jumbo) bags, leno mesh bags, HDPE tarpaulin, and dunnage bags , serving applications across agriculture, logistics, construction, and industrial sectors.

With a strong international presence, we are known as a dependable export partner, delivering solutions that ensure optimized storage, streamlined handling, and secure transit across global supply chains.
