U-Cut & W-Cut Non-Woven Bags for Retail & Packaging Applications

U-cut and W-cut non-woven bags are widely used for shopping, packaging, and promotional purposes due to their strong handle design, lightweight structure, and reusable nature. These bags are manufactured using high quality polypropylene non-woven fabric which provides excellent durability, flexibility, and cost effectiveness compared to traditional plastic carry bags.

Why Choose U-Cut & W-Cut Non-Woven Bags?

U-cut and W-cut non-woven bags are an excellent replacement for plastic carry bags. These bags are strong, reusable, and environmentally responsible packaging solutions. Their ergonomically designed cut handles provide better grip and make them ideal for retail shopping, supermarkets, and promotional use.

Our non-woven bags are manufactured using advanced ultrasonic sealing technology which ensures strong joints, smooth finishing, and high load carrying capacity. These bags can also be customized with multi-color printing for branding, advertising, and promotional activities.

  • Garment and textile packaging.
  • Grocery and supermarket bags.
  • Promotional and branding bags.
  • Exhibition and event packaging.
  • Pharmacy and medical store carry bags.
