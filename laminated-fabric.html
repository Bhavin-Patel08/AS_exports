Laminated Non Woven Fabric for Packaging & Industrial Applications

Laminated non woven fabric is widely used for packaging, medical, and surgical applications due to its strength, smooth surface, and excellent durability. The extrusion lamination process provides consistent soft quality, improved flexibility, and better affordability for various industrial applications.

Why Choose Laminated Non Woven Fabric?

Laminated non woven fabric offers superior strength and excellent surface finish compared to standard non-woven materials. The lamination layer improves water resistance and print quality, making it ideal for premium shopping bags and packaging products.

Our laminated fabrics are manufactured using advanced extrusion technology which ensures uniform thickness, better bonding strength, and smooth texture suitable for high quality printing and processing applications.
