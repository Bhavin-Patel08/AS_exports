Spunbond Non Woven Fabric Roll Manufacturer

Spunbond non-woven fabrics are sheet or web structures made by bonding fibers or filaments mechanically, thermally, or chemically. They are flat or porous sheets produced directly from fibers or molten plastic without weaving or knitting. These fabrics can include recycled materials depending on strength requirements and can also be recycled after proper treatment. Non-woven fabrics are considered eco-friendly for many applications, especially for disposable products used in hospitals, schools, nursing homes,and hospitality industries.

Spunbond Non-Woven Fabric Rolls for Industrial Applications

Spunbond Non-Woven Fabric Rolls are widely used in various industries due to their lightweight structure, durability, and cost-effective performance. These fabrics are manufactured using advanced spunbond technology which provides excellent strength, breathability, and flexibility for multiple industrial and commercial applications.

Why Choose Non-Woven Fabric Rolls?

Non-woven fabric rolls are becoming increasingly popular because of their versatility and eco-friendly properties. These fabrics offer excellent air permeability, moisture resistance, and softness, making them suitable for hygiene, medical, packaging, and agricultural applications. Compared to traditional woven fabrics, non-woven materials are more economical and easier to process.

Our spunbond non-woven fabrics are produced with high quality polypropylene fibers bonded through thermal processes. This ensures consistent fabric strength, smooth finish, and uniform thickness, making them ideal for both industrial use and consumer products.

Applications of Non-Woven Fabric Rolls

  • Widely used in medical and hygiene industries.
  • Used in agriculture for crop covers and plant protection.
  • Ideal for shopping bags and packaging products.
  • Used in furniture as sofa backing and mattress lining.
  • Suitable for filtration and insulation applications.
  • Used for protective clothing and PPE products.
