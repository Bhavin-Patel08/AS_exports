Spunbond Non Woven Fabric Roll Manufacturer

Spunbond non-woven fabrics are sheet or web structures made by bonding fibers or filaments mechanically, thermally, or chemically. They are flat or porous sheets produced directly from fibers or molten plastic without weaving or knitting. These fabrics can include recycled materials depending on strength requirements and can also be recycled after proper treatment. Non-woven fabrics are considered eco-friendly for many applications, especially for disposable products used in hospitals, schools, nursing homes,and hospitality industries.

  • Long Lasting and High Strength
  • Lightweight & Breathable
  • Eco-friendly Material
  • Water & Chemical Resistance

Used By Industries

Spunbond Non-Woven fabric is widely used in medical, packaging,agriculture, and furniture industries due to its strength and versatility. It is commonly used for surgical masks, shopping bags, crop covers, insulation layers, and mattress linings. Its cost-effectiveness and durability make it an ideal material for many industrial and commercial applications.
