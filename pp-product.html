Our Poly Packaging Product Range

AS Exports offers a wide range of industrial poly packaging products including PP woven bags, FIBC jumbo bags, HDPE tarpaulin sheets, sling bags, dunnage bags, leno mesh bags and woven fabric solutions used in agriculture, logistics and bulk material handling industries worldwide.

PP / HDPE Woven Sack Bags

High strength woven polypropylene bags widely used for packaging grains, fertilizer, cement and other bulk materials.

  • High tensile strength
  • Moisture resistant
  • Durable packaging solution

BOPP Laminated Bags

Attractive printed woven bags used for retail packaging of food grains, seeds, flour and consumer products.

  • High quality printing
  • Strong laminated layer
  • Retail packaging solution

FIBC Jumbo Bags

Flexible intermediate bulk containers designed for safe storage and transportation of bulk materials.

  • High load capacity
  • Industrial strength
  • Cost effective packaging

Sling Bags

Heavy duty lifting bags used for transporting and handling industrial bulk materials safely.

  • Strong lifting loops
  • Industrial grade fabric
  • Safe load handling

HDPE Tarpaulin Sheets

Waterproof tarpaulin sheets used for protection in agriculture, construction and transportation.

  • Waterproof protection
  • UV resistant
  • Outdoor durability

Inflatable Dunnage Bags

Air filled bags used to secure cargo and prevent movement during transportation.

  • Cargo protection
  • Reusable design
  • Easy installation

Leno Mesh Bags

Breathable mesh bags ideal for packaging onions, potatoes, garlic and vegetables.

  • Ventilated design
  • Lightweight material
  • Agricultural packaging

PP / HDPE Woven Fabric

High quality woven fabric rolls used for manufacturing bags, tarpaulin sheets and packaging materials.

  • High tensile strength
  • Moisture resistant
  • Durable packaging solution
