Crop Cover for Agriculture & Plant Protection

Crop cover is a high-performance non-woven polypropylene fabric designed to protect crops from harsh environmental conditions while supporting healthy plant growth. It acts as a protective barrier against insects, frost, wind, and excessive sunlight, making it ideal for modern farming practices.

Why Choose Crop Cover?

Crop cover fabric is widely used in agriculture for its ability to create a controlled microclimate around plants. It helps maintain temperature, reduce moisture loss, and protect crops without restricting airflow or sunlight.

Manufactured using advanced spunbond technology, our crop covers are lightweight, durable, and UV stabilized, ensuring long-term outdoor performance and consistent crop protection.

Applications of Crop Cover

  • Vegetable and fruit crop protection.
  • Nursery and horticulture applications.
  • Greenhouse and polyhouse farming.
  • Protection from frost and cold waves.
  • Insect and pest barrier.
  • Seed germination and early growth stages.

Features & Benefits of Crop Cover

  • UV stabilized for extended outdoor durability.
  • Allows air, water, and nutrients to pass through.
  • Maintains ideal temperature for plant growth.
  • Lightweight and easy to install.
  • Reduces need for chemical pesticides.
  • Reusable and eco-friendly solution.
