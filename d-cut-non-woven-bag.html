D-Cut Non-Woven Bags for Packaging & Carry Applications

D-cut non-woven bags are widely used for shopping, packaging, and promotional purposes due to their strong structure, lightweight material, and reusable nature. These bags are manufactured using high quality non-woven fabric which provides excellent durability, flexibility, and cost effectiveness compared to plastic bags.

Why Choose D-Cut Non-Woven Bags?

D-cut non-woven bags are an ideal replacement for plastic carry bags. These bags are strong, reusable, and environmentally friendly. Their simple design with die cut handles makes them convenient for daily shopping, retail stores, and promotional activities.

Our non-woven bags are manufactured using advanced ultrasonic sealing technology which ensures strong joints, smooth finishing, and excellent load carrying capacity. These bags can also be customized with printing for branding and promotional use.

Applications of D-Cut Non-Woven Bags

  • Shopping and retail carry bags.
  • Garment and textile packaging.
  • Grocery and supermarket bags.
  • Promotional and advertising bags.
  • Gift and exhibition packaging.
  • Pharmaceutical and medical store bags.

Features & Benefits of Our Non-Woven Bags

  • Reusable and eco-friendly alternative to plastic bags.
